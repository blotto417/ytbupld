Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ytbupld is a personal tool used only by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channels.

Data accessed

When you sign in with Google, ytbupld requests permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel, read basic channel information (channel ID, title, statistics), and read YouTube Analytics reports for that channel.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth refresh token and the analytics reports are stored only on the developer's local computer. They are used solely to upload videos chosen by the channel owner and to display the channel's own statistics. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

ytbupld's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ytbupld uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke ytbupld's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes stored access.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/blotto417/ytbupld/issues.
